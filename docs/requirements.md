# ACBB V1 Requirements

## Purpose

The website is a showcase and orientation site for Athletic Club Bernissart-Beloeil (ACBB). It serves prospective athletes and parents, existing athletes, young athletes, visitors, disability centres and institutions, volunteers, potential coaches, partners, and sponsors.

The site must remain concise, simple, mobile-first, accessible, understandable by non-technical visitors, and easy to maintain through future AI-assisted edits. Administrative and institutional content must remain easy to find without dominating primary navigation.

## Scope

### V1

- Home
- Training
- Club
- Competitions
- ACBB Events
- Join ACBB
- Handisport
- Clothing
- Club Life
- Announcements
- Become a Partner
- Contact
- Institutional and governance documents

### Future feature

Club records are planned for a later phase. They are not part of V1.

## Target Navigation

The proposed primary navigation is:

- Home
- Training
- Club
- Competitions
- ACBB Events

The primary call to action is "Join ACBB".

Administrative and institutional content should use contextual links and footer-accessible areas rather than competing for primary navigation.

## Home

The homepage is a showcase and orientation page, not a sitemap. It should provide concise access to:

- ACBB identity and introduction
- training groups
- joining the club
- Handisport
- upcoming competitions
- upcoming ACBB-organized events
- important active announcements
- lightweight Club Life content
- season sponsors and partners
- contextual contact and social access

Handisport must receive significant visual prominence, using substantial photography and concise accompanying text.

## Training

Training must support multiple groups and future groups without redesign.

Known domains include:

- youth athletics school
- middle-distance
- sprint, hurdles, and jumps
- Handisport

A training slot must support:

- group
- categories or birth years
- discipline
- day
- start time
- end time
- location
- one or more coaches
- optional note

The same coach may be assigned to several groups or disciplines. Training presentation must adapt between desktop and mobile without forcing a wide table onto small screens.

Training content must support pedagogical objectives based on DLTA information. An existing DLTA visual will be supplied later.

Current training schedules are pending and must not be invented.

## People

### Committee

### Coaches

Coach records must support:

- first name

Missing portraits must use a project-owned neutral fallback image. Do not duplicate fallback paths in individual person records.

The current coach and committee lists are pending.

## Join ACBB

### First affiliation

The intended journey is:

1. Attend up to two trial sessions.
2. Identify the appropriate training group.
3. Prepare the required documents.
4. Complete the athlete information form.
5. Obtain medical validation when required.
6. Submit documents according to the club procedure.
7. Pay the membership fee.
8. Complete or confirm the LBFA affiliation process.

Known documents include the athlete information form and a medical certificate. A declaration or attestation on honour is a possible requirement, but it remains TO CONFIRM. The final administrative procedure also remains TO CONFIRM until validated by the club.

### Re-affiliation

The renewal journey must support:

- season fee
- payment information
- required payment communication
- optional competition bib request
- LBFA or Twizzit confirmation

Season prices and dates must remain editable seasonal data rather than permanent presentation constants.

### Suspension or disaffiliation

The site must provide access to the appropriate information and documents, including separate adult and minor templates when applicable.

- item
- image
- description
- price
- sizes
- availability or stock status
- ordering instructions

No webshop is required. Prices, stock, and available items must remain easy to update. Current stock, prices, and photos are pending.

## Handisport

Handisport requires a dedicated page and prominent homepage visibility.

The Handisport page must support:

- section presentation
- authentic photography
- historical role of ACBB
- relationship or role with relevant athletics structures
- target audiences
- practical information
- training schedule
- coaches or supervisors
- dedicated contacts
- information for disability centres or institutions interested in joining or collaborating
- optional gallery

Detailed Handisport content, schedules, and institutional contacts remain pending and must not be invented.

## Competitions

Competitions must use structured data as the primary information source. Competition flyers must not be the only source of website information.

Upcoming competitions must support:

- date
- name
- discipline
- location
- concerned categories
- external link
- optional practical information
- complementary flyer or document images

Past competitions must support:

- short report
- notable ACBB results
- a small curated photo selection
- optional public Facebook post link

The V1 editorial format is short report, notable results, selected photos, and an optional Facebook link. Facebook embeds are not required.

## ACBB-Organized Events

The site must provide:

- a global season page listing events chronologically
- a dedicated page for each event

Event pages must support:

- title
- date
- location
- hero image or poster
- description
- distances
- schedules
- prices
- external registration link
- practical information
- routes
- GPX files
- regulations
- event-specific sponsors
- results after the event
- post-event report
- curated gallery
- public Facebook link

The same event URL must remain valid before and after the event. Known examples include La Dinosaurienne and 20 km de la Forêt de Belœil.
The event architecture must support adding future ACBB-organized events without requiring structural or component redesign.

## Club Life

V1 includes a lightweight Club Life area for occasional content such as club and youth activities, stages, awards, organisational news, and important club moments.

It is not a high-frequency blog. The architecture must tolerate little or no recent content without presenting an abandoned or broken experience.

## Announcements

Temporary announcements must support:

- title
- type
- publication date
- optional expiration date
- description
- contact or link
- active or inactive status

Active announcements may be surfaced on the homepage. Expired announcements must not require redesign to leave public display.

## Sponsors and Partners

The architecture must distinguish:

- season sponsors and partners
- event-specific sponsors and partners

Season partners should be eligible for homepage display. Event partners belong on the relevant event page. A partner may support both the season and one or more events without duplicating partner identity or logo.

A dedicated Become a Partner page is planned for V1 and should support:

- reasons to support ACBB
- season partnerships
- event partnerships
- material or service support
- current season partners
- partnership contact
- optional sponsorship PDF

Current partner data and partner-page content are pending.

## Contact

Use contextualized contacts for:

- general club or secretariat
- affiliation
- youth athletics
- Handisport
- sponsorship
- event-specific needs

Contact information is contextualized throughout the website rather than
duplicated in a dedicated Contact page.

Visitors must be able to identify the appropriate contact for each relevant
activity through the relevant club, affiliation, partnership, recruitment,
competition or event context.

V1 does not require a website contact form or backend.

## Social Media

The public Facebook Page may be linked from relevant public areas.

The private Facebook group intended for youth-school parent and coach communication may be linked only in appropriate youth or parent contexts. Private-group content must not be exposed.

The website remains the authoritative source for structured club information.

## Institutional and Governance Information

V1 must make the following easy to publish and discover without dominating primary navigation:

- basic club information
- training information
- pedagogical objectives or DLTA
- social-media links
- current competitions and activities calendar
- membership and registration procedures
- LBFA ethical charter
- organisation chart with board member names and functions
- internal regulations
- technical structure
- structured sports offer
- annual General Assembly minutes
- privacy or GDPR policy
- diversity and Handisport visibility
- volunteer and supervisor information
- dynamic youth, event, and result content

Prefer contextual links and a footer-accessible documents or governance area.

## Documents

The future architecture must group documents logically:

- Affiliation: athlete information form, medical certificate, and other confirmed documents
- Club: internal regulations, organisation chart, technical structure, and sports offer
- Ethics: LBFA ethical charter
- Governance: annual General Assembly minutes
- Privacy: privacy or GDPR policy
- Insurance: accident declaration
- Competitions and events: calendars, regulations, GPX files, and event documents

Real documents must not be integrated before they are supplied and validated.

## External Links

External links must be contextual rather than collected in a generic Useful Links page. Likely contexts include LBFA, ADEPS, Facebook, the private youth Facebook group, insurance resources, event registration forms, and event resources.

## Static Assets

`public/images/` contains website-displayable static assets, potentially covering branding, education, coaches, committee members, Handisport, facilities, competitions, events, Club Life, clothing, partners, and UI fallbacks.

`public/documents/` contains downloadable or reference documents, potentially covering affiliation, club, governance, ethics, privacy, insurance, competitions, and events.

No empty directories should be created solely for future asset domains. Generated `dist/` assets must not be maintained manually. Future project-managed asset names should be lowercase, descriptive, and hyphen-separated.

## Structured Content

Frequently changing business content must be separated from Astro presentation components whenever practical.

Future structured domains include:

- coaches
- committee
- training
- competitions
- events
- announcements
- partners
- clothing
- Club Life

The structured format will remain unselected unless a later decision is approved. These domains must be documented before implementation, not implemented in this change.

## Club Records — Phase 2

Club records are outside V1.

The future concept must support:

- category, starting from Cadet categories
- event or discipline
- athlete
- performance
- date
- location

Future submissions may use an external form. No record may be published automatically. The workflow must be athlete submission, human validation, then publication only when confirmed.
